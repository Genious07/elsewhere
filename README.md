## Red John’s Footsteps

[![Red John’s Footsteps — photographic title screen](previews/red-john.png)](https://elsewhere-satwik-web-lab.ctrlagent-4537.chatgpt.site/red-john.html)

A season of obsession, told through real portraits, a playable piano, a descent into the cellar and the choice that changes Patrick Jane’s pursuit. Includes an optional, credited interactive Jane sculpture.

**Explore:** season milestones, evidence, piano testimony, scroll-controlled 3D scenes and the complete finale ending. Spoilers through season one of *The Mentalist*.

## The Quiet Orbit

[![The Quiet Orbit — an illuminated sculptural world](previews/quiet-orbit.png)](https://elsewhere-satwik-web-lab.ctrlagent-4537.chatgpt.site/world.html)

Leave the familiar behind. Travel through floating stone and impossible circles toward a sun of your own. Original procedural forms respond to your scroll and pointer as the camera moves through the world.

**Explore:** four chapters, reversible camera movement, sculptural lighting, replay and a reduced-motion presentation.

## How the collection is organized

This repository is the front door: artwork, project links, descriptions and a machine-readable [project index](projects.json). The working website code lives in [elsewhere-experiences](https://github.com/Genious07/elsewhere-experiences). Future concepts can live in their own repositories and still appear here.

To add a finished concept:

1. Add its real demo URL, source URL and project details to `projects.json`.
2. Put a representative screenshot in `previews/` and add its credits to `CREDITS.md`.
3. Add a project entry above with demo, source and credits links.
4. Update `src/projects.ts` in the website repository when it should also appear in the live gallery.

List experiments only when they can be opened and explored. Keep prototypes in their own repositories until they are ready.
